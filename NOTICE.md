# Attribution and scope

The components were extracted and generalized from a project-local React UI implementation. The renderer uses Canvas 2D and the streaming cursor uses CSS. No paid export, shader, image, font, mascot or business asset is included.

As of v0.2.0, `solving` and `composing` adapt the MIT-licensed lattice and ribbon geometry from [Jakub Antalik's Libraries.dev thinking-orbs](https://github.com/Jakubantalik/Libraries.dev/tree/bcaf88f8a16a75fd95bf21e0747193ee3380a927/packages/thinking-orbs). The original copyright and full MIT license are retained in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). This is an adaptation, not an official distribution or affiliation.

The original visual references were the **band**, **ping** and **ribbon** thinking-orb effects in the [MetalForge editor](https://metalforge.xyz/editor#effect=thinking-orbs&style=band). These three are independent implementations, not an official MetalForge package or a promise of pixel-identical reproduction. The revised ping follows its public out-and-back spherical light-ring description; no paid shader export is used. The names describe visual variants here.

The default five-stop palette (cyan, blue, violet, red, orange) approximates a user-supplied Saint-Gobain brand mark for screen UI use. It is not an official brand specification.
